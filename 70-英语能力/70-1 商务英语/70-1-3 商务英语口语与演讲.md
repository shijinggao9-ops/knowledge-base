# 70-1-3 商务英语口语与演讲

**定位**：商务英语口语与演讲是商科生在职场中最“显性”的英语能力——会议发言、客户沟通、英文演讲、电话会议，都需要“说得清楚、说得得体”。与70-1-1的“词汇”和70-1-2的“写作”不同，这个文件解决的是“口头怎么表达”的问题：会议中怎么发言、演讲怎么组织、电话会议怎么应对、谈判怎么表达。掌握这些，你就能在英文工作场景中“开口不慌、表达有物”。

**适用场景**：英文会议发言、英文商务演讲、电话/视频会议、英文谈判、商务社交。


## 一、会议表达

### 1.1 会议主持

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 开场 | “Thanks everyone for joining. Let‘s get started.” | 简洁开场 |
| 介绍议程 | “Here’s what we‘ll cover today: first..., then..., and finally...” | 给听众路线图 |
| 切换到下一项 | “Moving on to the next item...” | 过渡 |
| 回到正题 | “Let’s get back on track.” | 讨论跑偏时使用 |
| 总结 | “To summarize today‘s discussion...” | 会议结尾 |
| 确认行动项 | “Let me confirm the action items:...” | 确保人人清楚 |
| 结束会议 | “Thanks everyone for your time.” | 简洁收尾 |

### 1.2 提问与回应

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 请求澄清 | “Just to clarify, are you saying that...” | 礼貌确认 |
| 请求重复 | “Could you please repeat that?” | 没听清时 |
| 表示同意 | “That’s a good point.” / “I completely agree.” | 肯定他人 |
| 表示部分同意 | “I see your point, but...” | 礼貌提出不同意见 |
| 表达不同意见 | “I see it a bit differently.” | 比直接说“I disagree”更得体 |
| 提出观点 | “From my perspective...” / “The way I see it...” | 引入个人观点 |
| 补充观点 | “I‘d like to add one more point.” | 补充发言 |
| 提问 | “I have a quick question on...” | 简练提问 |

### 1.3 礼貌打断

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 插话（礼貌）| “Sorry to interrupt, but I’d like to add...” | 礼貌打断 |
| 插话（谨慎）| “If I could just jump in for a moment...” | 更礼貌的打断 |
| 请对方继续 | “Sorry, please continue.” | 打断后让对方继续 |
| 确认轮到你了 | “Can I add something here?” | 确认发言时机 |

### 1.4 电话/视频会议

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 开场 | “Can everyone hear me clearly?” | 确认连线 |
| 连接问题 | “I think we lost you. Could you repeat that?” | 对方断线 |
| 请对方静音 | “Could you please mute your microphone?” | 背景噪音时 |
| 换人发言 | “I‘ll hand it over to [Name] now.” | 交接发言权 |
| 自己静音 | “I’m on mute — let me unmute.” | 提醒对方自己刚才静音了 |
| 结束 | “Thanks for joining everyone.” | 电话会议结尾 |


## 二、商务演讲

### 2.1 演讲的标准结构

商务演讲的结构与中文商务演讲一致，但语言表达有其特点。

1. Opening Hook（开场吸引）
    
2. Agenda（议程预览）
    
3. Main Content（主体内容）
    
4. Summary（总结）
    
5. Q&A（问答环节）

### 2.2 开场表达

**吸引注意力的三种方式**：

| 方式 | 句式 | 适用场景 |
|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| 数据开场 | “The revenue in Q3 grew 15% — but behind that number is a challenge we need to address.” | 汇报、提案 |
| 问题开场 | “What if we could reduce customer acquisition costs by 30% while improving retention?” | 提案、路演 |
| 故事开场 | “Let me start with a story about one of our customers...” | 路演、分享 |

**开场通用句式**：

> “Good morning everyone. Thank you for being here today.”
> “I‘m here today to talk about...”
> “Let me start with a quick overview of...”
> “I’ll keep this brief and focus on three key points.”

### 2.3 议程预览

> “Here‘s what we’ll cover today. First, I‘ll walk you through our Q3 performance. Then, we’ll look at the key drivers. Finally, I‘ll share our Q4 priorities.”
> “I’ve divided this presentation into three parts. Part one is..., part two is..., and part three is...”

### 2.4 过渡与衔接

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 结束一部分 | “That covers part one.” | 小结 |
| 开始新部分 | “Moving on to part two...” | 过渡 |
| 深挖 | “Let me drill down into this a bit more.” | 深入细节 |
| 回到高层面 | “Stepping back to the big picture...” | 回到全局 |
| 引出图表 | “Let‘s take a look at this chart.” | 图表展示 |
| 强调重点 | “This is the key takeaway...” | 突出核心 |

### 2.5 描述数据

| 趋势 | 句式 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 上升 | “X increased / grew / rose / climbed / surged by...” | “Revenue surged by 22%.” |
| 下降 | “X decreased / declined / dropped / fell / slid by...” | “Costs declined by 8%.” |
| 稳定 | “X remained stable / held steady / plateaued.” | “Market share held steady.” |
| 波动 | “X fluctuated between... and...” | “Sales fluctuated between $1M and $1.2M.” |
| 峰值 | “X peaked at... / reached a high of...” | “Revenue peaked at $1.4M in June.” |
| 谷值 | “X hit a low of... / bottomed out at...” | “Sales bottomed out at $800K.” |
| 占比 | “X accounted for... / made up... / represented...” | “East China accounted for 60% of total revenue.” |
| 对比 | “X vs Y / X compared to Y / while X, Y...” | “While Q3 grew 15%, Q4 only grew 5%.” |

### 2.6 结尾

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 总结 | “Let me quickly summarize the key points...” | 回顾核心内容 |
| 金句 | “So the bottom line is...” / “In short...” | 精炼收尾 |
| 行动呼吁 | “I encourage you to...” / “I’d like to propose we...” | 明确行动 |
| 开放问答 | “That concludes my presentation. I‘m happy to take any questions.” | Q&A环节 |


## 三、谈判与说服

### 3.1 提出方案

| 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “We propose the following approach...” | 正式提出方案 |
| “Our recommendation is to...” | 提出建议 |
| “What if we...” | 启发式提问 |
| “I’d suggest we consider...” | 礼貌建议 |
| “One option we‘re exploring is...” | 不完全确定的方案 |

### 3.2 回应反对意见

| 场景 | 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 先认可 | “I understand your concern, and that’s a valid point.” | 先建立共识 |
| 再回应 | “However, let me explain why we think this approach works...” | 给出理由 |
| 提供解决方案 | “To address that concern, we suggest...” | 解决具体问题 |
| 若暂时无法回应 | “That‘s a good point. Let me get back to you on that.” | 后续跟进 |

### 3.3 争取时间思考

| 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “That’s an interesting question. Let me think about it.” | 礼貌争取时间 |
| “I don‘t have that number at hand, but I’ll follow up with the details.” | 后续提供 |
| “Let me circle back on this after we‘ve looked at the data.” | 暂缓讨论 |

### 3.4 确认共识

| 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “So we’re aligned on the next steps, correct?” | 确认一致 |
| “Let me make sure I‘ve understood correctly. You’re saying that...” | 确认理解 |
| “Are we in agreement on this?” | 确认共识 |


## 四、商务社交（Networking）

### 4.1 自我介绍（商务场合）

| 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “Hi, I‘m [Name] from [Company/Team].” | 简单明了 |
| “I’m currently leading [role/responsibility] at [Company].” | 说明角色 |
| “What brings you to this event?” | 开场白——让对方开口 |
| “I‘ve been following your work on [topic].” | 建立连接——让对方感到你关注过他们 |

### 4.2 结束对话

| 句式 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “It was great talking to you.” | 礼貌结束 |
| “Let’s connect on LinkedIn.” | 后续跟进 |
| “I‘ll be sure to reach out next week to follow up.” | 明确后续动作 |
| “Let me grab your card / WeChat.” | 交换联系方式 |


## 五、发音与流畅度提示

### 5.1 关键商务词汇的重音

| 词汇 | 正确重音位置 | 错误常见重音 |
|:-----|:------------|:------------|
| de**vel**op | 第2音节 | DEvelop（第1音节）|
| **an**a-lyze | 第1音节（也可读作第1音节）| anaLYZE（第3音节）|
| **strate**gy | 第1音节 | straTEGy（第2音节）|
| fi**nance** | 第2音节 | FI-nance（第1音节）|
| **pro**duct | 第1音节 | proDUCT（第2音节）|

### 5.2 流畅度提升技巧

| 技巧 | 说明 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| **填充词** | 给自己思考时间 | “Let me put it this way...”、“Well, I think...” |
| **换种说法** | 卡住时换说法 | “What I‘m trying to say is...” |
| **确认理解** | 确保对方跟上 | “Does that make sense?” |
| **简化表达** | 不确定语法时用简单句 | 不说复杂从句，用短句表达 |


## 二、商业场景还原

### 场景1：会议发言

**情境**：项目评审会上，你需要回应关于项目延期的质疑。

> “That’s a fair concern, and I completely understand the need to stay on schedule.
>
> We did fall behind by two weeks in the development phase. The main reason was an unexpected dependency on the third-party API integration. However, we’ve already resolved that issue and we‘re back on track.
>
> To ensure we catch up, we’ve added two developers to the team for the next three weeks. We still expect to deliver by the original deadline.
>
> I‘ll be happy to share a more detailed timeline if that would be helpful.”

### 场景2：商务演讲片段

**情境**：向管理团队汇报Q3业绩，开场部分。

> “Good morning everyone. Thanks for making time today.
>
> In the first half of this year, we were facing flat growth and rising costs. The question was: how do we turn this around?
>
> I’m happy to report that Q3 performance tells a different story. Revenue grew 15% year-over-year, with East China leading the way at 22% growth. Costs are down 5% from last quarter.
>
> Over the next 15 minutes, I‘ll walk you through three key drivers of this turnaround, and then we’ll discuss the implications for Q4.“

### 场景3：电话会议应对技术问题

**情境**：电话会议中，对方听不清你说话。

> “I think we might have a connection issue. Can you still hear me clearly? Okay, great. Let me repeat the last part to make sure everyone caught it...”


## 三、实战自测题

- [ ] **会议发言演练**：你正在参加一个英文项目评审会，老板问“Why is this project running late?”。请用英文即兴回答（30-60秒），包含：原因、已采取的措施、新的时间预期。

- [ ] **演讲开场设计**：你需要在英文汇报中介绍“Q4增长策略”，请分别写出“数据开场”“问题开场”“故事开场”三个版本的英文开场白（各1-2句）。

- [ ] **数据描述练习**：请用英文描述以下趋势（用至少3种不同的表达方式）：某公司销售额从Q1的$1.0M增长到Q2的$1.3M，Q3达到$1.5M，Q4小幅回落到$1.45M。

- [ ] **模拟电话会议**：你在英文电话会议中，因为信号问题漏掉了对方说的关键信息。请设计一段你与对方的对话，包含：请求重复、确认理解、继续话题。
