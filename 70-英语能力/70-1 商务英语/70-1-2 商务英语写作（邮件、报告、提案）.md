**定位**：商务写作是商科生最频繁的英文输出场景——邮件要写得清晰得体，报告要写得专业有据，提案要写得有说服力。与70-1-1的“词汇与表达”相比，这个文件解决的是“整篇怎么写”的问题：结构怎么搭、语气怎么定、内容怎么组织。掌握这些，你就能在英文邮件、报告、提案中写出“让对方读得懂、信得过、愿意回”的内容。

**适用场景**：撰写英文商务邮件、英文工作报告、英文方案提案、英文项目汇报。


## 一、商务邮件写作

### 1.1 邮件的核心原则

| 原则 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| **主题行 = 核心信息** | 收件人扫一眼就知道“什么事” |
| **第一句 = 核心结论** | 不要铺垫，直接说重点 |
| **每段一个主题** | 段落控制在3-5行，便于快速阅读 |
| **行动明确** | 对方看完后知道“我要做什么、什么时候做” |
| **语气得体** | 根据收件人调整正式程度 |

### 1.2 邮件的基本结构

Subject: [行动请求/核心结论]

[称呼]，

[第一段：核心结论 + 行动请求]  
[第二段：背景/原因/支撑信息（2-3句）]  
[第三段：明确的时间节点和下一步]

[结尾：礼貌收尾 + 签名]


### 1.3 常见邮件场景模板

#### 场景一：请示/审批邮件

> **Subject**: Approval Request: Q4 Marketing Budget
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> I‘d like to request your approval on the Q4 marketing budget proposal.
>
> The total budget is $180K, which is a 15% increase from Q3, primarily driven by additional investment in short-video campaigns. The expected ROI is 1:3.5, up from 1:3.0 in Q3.
>
> Could you please review the attached proposal and let me know your decision by this Thursday, August 10?
>
> Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

#### 场景二：同步/汇报邮件

> **Subject**: Q3 Sales Performance Update
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Q3 revenue came in at $12M, exceeding our target of $11.6M by 3%.
>
> Key drivers:
> - East China grew 22% YoY, accounting for 60% of total growth
> - New product line contributed an additional 5% growth
> - Customer retention remained stable at 85%
>
> One area of concern: customer satisfaction dropped 1 point to 84/100. We're investigating the root cause and will share findings next week.
>
> Please let me know if you need more details.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

#### 场景三：跟进/催办邮件

> **Subject**: Follow-up: Q4 Marketing Budget Approval
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on my previous email regarding the Q4 marketing budget approval.
>
> We‘re aiming to finalize the plan by this Friday to ensure a smooth launch in October. If you have any concerns about the proposal, I’d be happy to address them.
>
> Could you please get back to me by end of today? If you need more time, please let me know.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

#### 场景四：拒绝/婉拒邮件（礼貌但清晰）

> **Subject**: Re: Partnership Proposal
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> Thank you for sharing the partnership proposal. We appreciate the time and effort your team has put into it.
>
> After careful review, we‘ve decided not to move forward with this opportunity at this time, as it doesn’t fully align with our current strategic priorities.
>
> We hope to keep in touch and explore potential collaboration in the future.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

#### 场景五：会议邀请与确认

> **Subject**: Meeting Invitation: Q4 Strategy Review
>
> Hi everyone,
>
> I‘d like to schedule a meeting to review our Q4 strategy.
>
> Agenda:
> 1. Q3 performance recap (10min)
> 2. Q4 priorities and resource allocation (20min)
> 3. Key risks and mitigation (10min)
> 4. Open discussion (10min)
>
> Proposed time: Thursday, Aug 12, 10:00-11:00 AM
> Please let me know if this time works for you.
>
> Looking forward to the discussion.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

### 1.4 邮件语气控制

| 语气 | 适用场景 | 特征 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| **正式** | 客户、高管、外部合作伙伴 | 完整句式、礼貌用语、不缩写 |
| **半正式** | 日常上级、跨部门同事 | 适度缩写、礼貌但简洁 |
| **非正式** | 团队内部、熟悉同事 | 缩写、简洁、可直接 |

**正式程度对比**：

| 正式 | 半正式 | 非正式 |
|:-----|:-------|:-------|
| “I would like to request...” | “I‘d like to request...” | “Can you...” |
| “Please find attached...” | “Attached is...” | “Here’s the file...” |
| “At your earliest convenience” | “By end of week” | “ASAP” |
| “I am writing to inform you...” | “Just a quick update...” | “Quick update...” |


## 二、商务报告写作

### 2.1 报告的核心原则

| 原则 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| **Executive Summary优先** | 管理层可能只读第一页 |
| **数据支撑结论** | 每个结论背后都有数据 |
| **可视化优先** | 图表 > 表格 > 文字 |
| **建议明确** | 报告不只是“分析”，更是“建议” |

### 2.2 商务报告的标准结构

1. Title Page（标题页）
    
2. Executive Summary（执行摘要）
    
3. Table of Contents（目录）
    
4. Introduction / Background（引言/背景）
    
5. Findings / Analysis（发现/分析）
    
6. Recommendations（建议）
    
7. Conclusion（结论）
    
8. Appendix（附录）


### 2.3 各部分写作要点

#### Executive Summary（执行摘要）

**目的**：让读者在2分钟内了解报告的核心内容。

**内容**：
- 1句话：报告的目的是什么
- 2-3句话：核心发现是什么
- 1-2句话：建议是什么

**示例**：
> This report analyzes the competitive landscape of China‘s ready-to-eat meal market and evaluates our potential entry strategy. Key findings show the market is growing at 15% annually with low concentration (CR5 < 20%), presenting a significant opportunity. However, entry barriers in supply chain and channel distribution remain high. We recommend a phased entry approach, starting with a pilot in East China before scaling to national coverage.

#### Findings / Analysis（发现/分析）

**要点**：
- 先给结论，再给数据
- 每个主要发现单独成段
- 用图表辅助说明

**语言特点**：
- “The data suggests that...”
- “Our analysis indicates...”
- “A closer look at X reveals...”
- “There is a clear correlation between X and Y.”

#### Recommendations（建议）

**要点**：
- 建议要具体、可执行
- 每个建议附带“预期效果”
- 建议要有优先级排序

**示例**：
> 1. **Short-term (Q4 2026)**: Optimize customer service response time to under 2 hours. Expected impact: reduce churn by 3 percentage points.
> 2. **Medium-term (H1 2027)**: Launch a customer loyalty program. Expected impact: increase repeat purchase rate by 10%.
> 3. **Long-term (2028+)**: Develop an AI-powered recommendation engine. Expected impact: improve cross-sell conversion by 15%.

### 2.4 描述数据的英语表达

| 趋势 | 表达方式 | 示例 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| 上升 | increase, rise, grow, climb, surge | “Revenue grew by 15% YoY.” |
| 下降 | decrease, decline, drop, fall, slide | “Costs declined by 8%.” |
| 稳定 | remain stable, hold steady, plateau | “Market share held steady at 25%.” |
| 波动 | fluctuate, vary, show volatility | “Sales fluctuated between $1M and $1.2M.” |
| 对比 | in contrast, while, whereas, compared to | “While Q3 revenue grew 15%, Q4 only grew 5%.” |
| 占比 | account for, represent, make up | “East China accounts for 60% of total revenue.” |
| 达到 | reach, hit, amount to, total | “Revenue reached $12M in Q3.” |


## 三、商务提案写作

### 3.1 提案的核心原则

| 原则 | 说明 |
|:-----|:-----|
| **问题先行** | 先让对方意识到“这是个问题”，再给出方案 |
| **方案 = 问题 + 答案** | 每个方案要点都与前面的问题对应 |
| **效果可量化** | 不要只说“会变好”，要说“会变好多少” |
| **行动明确** | 对方看完后知道“要做什么、谁来做、什么时候做” |

### 3.2 商务提案的标准结构


1. Title Page（标题页）
    
2. Executive Summary（执行摘要）
    
3. Problem Statement（问题陈述）
    
4. Proposed Solution（方案描述）
    
5. Expected Outcomes（预期效果）
    
6. Implementation Plan（实施计划）
    
7. Budget & Resources（预算与资源）
    
8. Call to Action（行动请求）

### 3.3 各部分写作要点

#### Problem Statement（问题陈述）

**要点**：用数据和事实说明“问题确实存在、而且很严重”。

**示例**：
> Currently, our customer retention rate stands at 68%, down from 75% last year. Analysis shows that 60% of churned customers cite “poor post-purchase support” as their primary reason for leaving. This is costing us approximately $500K annually in lost revenue. If no action is taken, we project retention could drop to 60% by the end of 2027.

#### Proposed Solution（方案描述）

**要点**：方案要“对应用户的问题”，不要自说自话。

**示例**：
> We propose establishing a dedicated Customer Success team focused on post-purchase engagement. Key components:
> - **Proactive support**: Regular check-ins with high-value customers
> - **Usage analytics**: Identify at-risk customers early
> - **Retention campaigns**: Targeted re-engagement for dormant accounts

#### Expected Outcomes（预期效果）

**要点**：量化效果，给决策者“信心”。

**示例**：
> We expect the following outcomes within 6 months:
> - Retention rate: 68% → 80% (+12pp)
> - Customer satisfaction: 82 → 88 (+6 points)
> - Incremental revenue: Estimated $400K annually

#### Call to Action（行动请求）

**示例**：
> We request approval to:
> - Hire 2 Customer Success Managers (Q4 2026)
> - Allocate $30K for Customer Success software
> - Launch the program by October 2026


## 二、商业场景还原

### 场景1：英文邮件改写

**原始中文邮件**：
> “张总您好，关于上次我们讨论的那个项目的事情，我想跟您确认一下时间表。我们这边计划是下个月15号启动，需要您那边确认一下资源能不能到位。如果可以的话，我们就开始推进了。谢谢。”

**改写后英文邮件**：

> **Subject**: Project Timeline Confirmation
>
> Dear Mr. Zhang,
>
> I‘d like to confirm the timeline for the project we discussed earlier.
>
> We plan to launch on the 15th of next month. Could you please confirm whether the required resources will be available by that date? Once confirmed, we’ll start execution immediately.
>
> Please let me know by end of this week if possible.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your Name]

### 场景2：英文报告片段

**情境**：季度业绩汇报中，需要描述Q3表现。

> **Q3 Performance Summary**
>
> Q3 revenue came in at $12.2M, exceeding our target of $11.6M by 5%. Year-over-year growth was 15%, driven primarily by strong performance in East China (+22%) and the successful launch of our new product line.
>
> However, customer satisfaction declined from 85 to 82, largely due to longer response times in customer support. This is a risk to future retention and requires immediate attention.
>
> Net income for the quarter was $1.5M, up 20% from Q2, reflecting improved cost efficiency across all business units.

### 场景3：英文提案摘要

**情境**：向管理层提案“上线会员积分系统”的摘要部分。

> **Executive Summary**
>
> Customer retention has been declining steadily over the past six months, dropping from 75% to 68%. To address this, we propose launching a loyalty points program designed to reward repeat purchases and increase customer lifetime value.
>
> Key benefits:
> - Increase retention by 8-10 percentage points within 6 months
> - Improve customer lifetime value by 15%
> - Reduce dependency on promotional discounts
>
> The program requires an initial investment of $80K and is expected to break even within 4 months. We recommend a Q4 launch to capture the holiday season.


## 三、常见错误与避坑

| 错误 | 正确写法 | 说明 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| “We will revert back to you.” | “We will revert to you.” | revert已经包含“back”的意思 |
| “I am agree with you.” | “I agree with you.” | agree是动词，不需要am |
| “The solution is viable? I think yes.” | “The solution is viable in my view.” | 正式场合避免口语化表达 |
| “Please revert at the earliest.” | “Please respond at your earliest convenience.” | revert通常指“恢复原状”或“回到前一个状态”，不直接等同“回复邮件”；此处建议用“respond”或“get back” |
| “We look forward to see you.” | “We look forward to seeing you.” | look forward to + ing |
| “According to me...” | “From my perspective...” / “In my view...” | “according to”一般不用于第一人称自我引用 |

- [ ] **提案写作**：你希望向老板提案“增设一个数据分析师岗位”。请写出提案中的“Problem Statement”和“Expected Outcomes”两个部分。
