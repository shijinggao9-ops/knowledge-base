# 70-1-1 商务英语核心词汇与表达

**定位**：商务英语不是“高级英语”，而是“在商业场景中准确、得体、高效地使用英语”。这个文件覆盖商务场景中最常用的词汇、短语、句式和表达方式——不是让你“背单词”，而是让你“知道在什么场景用什么词”。理解这些，你就能在邮件、会议、面试中避免“词不达意”的尴尬。

**适用场景**：撰写英文邮件、参加英文会议、英文面试、阅读英文商业材料、日常职场英文沟通。


## 一、核心商务动词与短语

以下动词和短语是商务英语中出现频率最高的——掌握它们，就能覆盖80%以上的日常商务沟通。

### 1.1 沟通与跟进类

| 动词/短语 | 含义 | 例句 | 使用场景 |
|:----------|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **reach out (to)** | 主动联系某人 | “I‘ll reach out to the client to schedule a meeting.” | 发起联系 |
| **follow up (on)** | 跟进某事 | “Just following up on my previous email.” | 邮件/电话跟进 |
| **circle back (on)** | 稍后回来讨论 | “Let’s circle back on this after the Q3 results.” | 会议/讨论中 |
| **touch base (with)** | 简短沟通/同步 | “Let‘s touch base next week to align on the plan.” | 日常沟通 |
| **get back (to)** | 回复某人 | “I’ll get back to you with the numbers by EOD.” | 承诺回复 |
| **keep (someone) posted** | 保持同步/告知进展 | “We‘ll keep you posted on the progress.” | 项目同步 |
| **loop (someone) in** | 把某人加入邮件/讨论 | “Let’s loop in the legal team on this.” | 跨部门协作 |
| **sync up (with)** | 对齐/同步信息 | “We need to sync up before the client meeting.” | 团队对齐 |

### 1.2 决策与行动类

| 动词/短语 | 含义 | 例句 | 使用场景 |
|:----------|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **sign off (on)** | 批准/签字同意 | “The VP needs to sign off on the budget.” | 审批流程 |
| **greenlight** | 批准/给绿灯 | “We‘re waiting for the board to greenlight the project.” | 项目启动 |
| **push back (on)** | 反对/推迟/施压 | “The client pushed back on the timeline.” | 谈判/讨论 |
| **table (a topic)** | 暂缓讨论 | “Let’s table this discussion until next quarter.” | 会议管理 |
| **drill down (into)** | 深入分析 | “Let‘s drill down into the numbers.” | 数据分析 |
| **scope out** | 评估/探索可行性 | “We’re scoping out the potential of entering the SEA market.” | 前期调研 |
| **roll out** | 推出/上线 | “We plan to roll out the new feature in October.” | 产品发布 |
| **phase in/out** | 逐步引入/逐步淘汰 | “We‘ll phase out the old system gradually.” | 变革管理 |

### 1.3 进度与状态类

| 动词/短语 | 含义 | 例句 | 使用场景 |
|:----------|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **on track** | 按计划推进 | “The project is on track for the October launch.” | 项目汇报 |
| **fall behind (on)** | 进度落后 | “We’re falling behind on the development schedule.” | 风险汇报 |
| **catch up (on)** | 赶上进度 | “We need to catch up on the backlog.” | 补救措施 |
| **wrap up** | 完成/收尾 | “Let‘s wrap up this meeting in five minutes.” | 会议/项目结束 |
| **kick off** | 启动/开始 | “We’ll kick off the project next Monday.” | 项目启动 |
| **put together** | 组织/整理 | “Can you put together a report by Friday?” | 任务分配 |

### 1.4 财务与资源类

| 动词/短语 | 含义 | 例句 | 使用场景 |
|:----------|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **allocate (to)** | 分配资源/预算 | “We’ve allocated 30% of the budget to marketing.” | 预算管理 |
| **run (numbers/models)** | 跑数据/做模型 | “Could you run the numbers for the Q4 forecast?” | 数据分析 |
| **come in (at/under)** | 最终结果是（数字）| “The Q3 revenue came in at $1.2M.” | 业绩汇报 |
| **ballpark (figure)** | 大致估算 | “Just give me a ballpark figure.” | 初期讨论 |
| **in the red/black** | 亏损/盈利 | “We‘re in the black for the first time this year.” | 财务状况 |


## 二、邮件常用句式

商务邮件需要“结构清晰、语气得当、行动明确”。以下句式覆盖了邮件中最常见的场景。

### 2.1 开场与问候

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 初次联系 | “I hope this email finds you well.” | 传统开场，可用于初次联系 |
| 已知联系人 | “Hope you’re having a good week.” | 更轻松的问候 |
| 跟进邮件 | “Following up on my previous email regarding...” | 礼貌跟进 |
| 快速切入 | “I‘m writing to follow up on...” | 直接切入主题 |

### 2.2 请求与行动

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 请求信息 | “Could you please provide...?” | 最礼貌的请求 |
| 请求行动 | “Could you please take a look at... and get back to me by...” | 明确时间 |
| 分配任务 | “I’d like you to take the lead on...” | 分配任务 |
| 确认收到 | “Could you please confirm receipt of...” | 确认送达 |

### 2.3 附件与参考

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 附上文件 | “Please find attached...” | 正式说法 |
| 附上文件（简洁）| “Attached is...” | 更简洁的说法 |
| 参考链接 | “For more details, please refer to...” | 引用外部资料 |
| 参见下文 | “Please see below for...” | 正文中的引用 |

### 2.4 截止时间与紧急程度

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 明确的截止时间 | “Please respond by EOD Friday.” | EOD = End of Day |
| 紧急 | “This is urgent. Please prioritize.” | 谨慎使用——不要滥用 |
| 确认截止时间 | “We’re targeting a Q4 launch.” | 可用于大型项目节点 |
| 提醒 | “Just a gentle reminder that...” | 礼貌提醒 |

### 2.5 结尾与落款

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 正式结尾 | “Looking forward to your reply.” | 最常用的结尾 |
| 行动性结尾 | “Please let me know if you have any questions.” | 鼓励对方回应 |
| 正式签名 | “Sincerely,” / “Best regards,” | 正式程度依次递减 |
| 简洁签名 | “Best,” / “Regards,” | 日常邮件常用 |


## 三、会议常用表达

### 3.1 会议主持

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 开场 | “Thanks everyone for joining. Let’s get started.” | 简洁开场 |
| 介绍议程 | “Here‘s what we’ll cover today...” | 议程预览 |
| 过渡 | “Moving on to the next point...” | 切换话题 |
| 总结 | “To summarize today‘s discussion...” | 会议结尾 |
| 确认行动项 | “Let me confirm the action items...” | 确保清晰 |

### 3.2 提问与回应

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 请求澄清 | “Just to clarify, are you saying that...?” | 礼貌澄清 |
| 表示同意 | “That’s a good point.” / “I completely agree.” | 有建设性的同意 |
| 表示不同意（礼貌）| “I see it a bit differently...” / “I understand, but...” | 不直接说“不同意” |
| 提出观点 | “From my perspective...” / “The way I see it is...” | 提出个人观点 |
| 打断（礼貌）| “Sorry to interrupt, but I‘d like to add...” | 礼貌打断 |

### 3.3 电话/视频会议

| 场景 | 句式 | 备注 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 连接问题 | “I think we lost you. Can you repeat that?” | 技术问题 |
| 换人发言 | “I’ll hand it over to Anna now.” | 交接发言权 |
| 不清晰 | “Can you please unmute yourself?” | 静音问题 |


## 四、商务缩写速查表

| 缩写 | 全称 | 含义 | 使用场景 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **ASAP** | As Soon As Possible | 尽快 | 任务分配 |
| **EOD** | End of Day | 今天下班前 | 截止时间 |
| **COB** | Close of Business | 下班前（约5-6点）| 截止时间 |
| **EOW** | End of Week | 本周内 | 截止时间 |
| **FYI** | For Your Information | 供参考 | 邮件/转发 |
| **FYR** | For Your Reference | 供参考 | 附件/转发 |
| **TBD** | To Be Determined | 待定 | 计划/决策 |
| **TBC** | To Be Confirmed | 待确认 | 计划/决策 |
| **P&L** | Profit and Loss | 损益（表）| 财务 |
| **ROI** | Return on Investment | 投资回报率 | 财务/评估 |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator | 关键绩效指标 | 管理/运营 |
| **OKR** | Objectives and Key Results | 目标与关键成果 | 管理/战略 |
| **NDA** | Non-Disclosure Agreement | 保密协议 | 法律/商务 |
| **MOU** | Memorandum of Understanding | 谅解备忘录 | 合作/协议 |
| **Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4** | Quarter 1-4 | 第一季度至第四季度 | 时间/计划 |
| **H1/H2** | First/Second Half | 上半年/下半年 | 时间/计划 |
| **EOD/EOW/EOQ** | End of Day/Week/Quarter | 日/周/季度末 | 截止时间 |


## 五、正式 vs 非正式表达对比

商务英语的关键能力之一，是“根据场景选择正确的正式程度”。

| 正式（外对客户/高管汇报）| 非正式（团队内部/日常沟通）|
|:------------------------|:------------------------|
| “We anticipate...” | “We think...” |
| “I would like to request...” | “Can you please...” |
| “We are currently evaluating...” | “We‘re looking at...” |
| “In accordance with...” | “Following...” |
| “Please find attached...” | “Attached is...” |
| “At your earliest convenience” | “When you get a chance” |
| “I am writing to inform you that...” | “Just a quick update...” |


## 六、常见“中式英语”误区

| ❌ 中式英语 | ✅ 地道商务英语 | 说明 |
|:-----------|:---------------|:-----|
| “We will do this as soon as possible” | “We’ll prioritize this.” | 更直接 |
| “How to say in English?” | “How do you say this in English?” | 语法修正 |
| “I will feedback you later.” | “I‘ll give you feedback later.” | 不是动词 |
| “We need to discuss about this.” | “We need to discuss this.” | discuss是及物动词 |
| “The price is too expensive.” | “The price is too high.” | 价格用high/low |
| “Please kindly find...” | “Please find...” | “kindly”和“please”重复 |
| “According to me...” | “From my perspective...” | 根据不说“according to me” |
| “I very like this proposal.” | “I really like this proposal.” | “very like”不是英文 |


## 七、实战自测题

- [ ] **邮件改写**：将以下中文邮件内容翻译为地道的商务英文邮件：
  > “我想跟进一下上周客户会议的事情。客户对我们的方案还有一些疑问，尤其是价格方面。麻烦您看看客户的反馈，然后我们约个时间讨论一下应对策略。”

- [ ] **场景填空**：请用正确的商务动词或短语填空：
  - （1）“We need to _____ the new feature in November.”（推出）
  - （2）“Let’s _____ this discussion until next week.”（暂缓）
  - （3）“Could you please _____ the marketing team on this email?”（加入邮件）

- [ ] **正式程度判断**：以下场景应该用“正式”还是“非正式”表达？
  - （1）向大客户CEO发送项目进度邮件
  - （2）给团队同事发内部协作消息
  - （3）向董事会提交季度报告
  - （4）与供应商确认交付时间
