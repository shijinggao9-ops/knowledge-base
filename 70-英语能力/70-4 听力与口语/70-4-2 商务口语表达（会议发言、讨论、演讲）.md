**定位**：商务口语表达是商科生在国际化工作场景中最核心的输出能力——英文会议中要清晰表达观点、参与讨论要自然得体、演讲要有说服力。这个文件帮你建立“怎么说”的方法论：会议发言的结构化表达、讨论中的参与技巧、演讲的组织逻辑、即兴发言的应对框架。不是教你“背句子”，而是教你“在不同场景下有结构地表达观点”。

**适用场景**：英文工作会议发言、参与小组讨论、英文汇报与演讲、即兴发言与回应。


## 一、会议发言

### 1.1 会议发言的核心结构

好的会议发言遵循一个简单的结构：**观点 → 理由 → 例子/数据 → 结论（重申观点）**

| 步骤 | 内容 | 句式示例 |
|:----:|:-----|:---------|
| 1 | 提出观点 | “I think we should focus on...” |
| 2 | 给出理由 | “The main reason is...” |
| 3 | 提供支撑 | “For example, last quarter...” |
| 4 | 重申结论 | “So my view is that...” |

### 1.2 提出观点

| 句式 | 正式程度 | 适用场景 |
|:-----|:--------:|:---------|
| “I think we should...” | 中等 | 日常讨论 |
| “I believe the best approach is...” | 中等 | 表达信念 |
| “From my perspective...” | 中等 | 强调个人视角 |
| “I would suggest that we...” | 中等 | 提出建议 |
| “It might be worth considering...” | 正式 | 委婉提议 |
| “I’d like to propose that...” | 正式 | 正式提案 |
| “My recommendation would be...” | 正式 | 给出建议 |

### 1.3 补充观点

当他人发言后，你想补充时：

| 句式 | 用法 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “Building on what [Name] said...” | 在他人观点上补充 |
| “I’d like to add one more point...” | 添加新点 |
| “To add to that...” | 补充说明 |
| “Following up on that...” | 跟进上一个观点 |
| “Another thing to consider is...” | 提出额外考量 |

### 1.4 回应他人

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 完全同意 | “I completely agree.” / “That‘s a great point.” |
| 部分同意 | “I see your point, but...” / “That’s true, however...” |
| 不同意（礼貌）| “I see it a bit differently...” / “I understand, but I think...” |
| 需要澄清 | “Could you clarify what you mean by...?” |

### 1.5 会议中的“礼貌打断”

| 句式 | 正式程度 |
|:-----|:--------:|
| “Sorry to interrupt, but...” | 中等 |
| “If I could just jump in...” | 中等 |
| “Can I add something here?” | 中等 |
| “I’d like to interject for a moment...” | 正式 |


## 二、讨论参与

### 2.1 如何切入讨论

当你想加入正在进行的讨论时：

| 句式 | 用法 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “Can I share my thoughts on this?” | 请求发言 |
| “I have a quick question about...” | 提问切入 |
| “I‘d like to chime in on this.” | 加入讨论 |
| “Can I ask a follow-up question?” | 追问切入 |

### 2.2 如何推进讨论

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 引导回到正题 | “Let’s get back to the main point.” |
| 推进决策 | “Do we have a consensus on this?” |
| 确认理解 | “Just to make sure I understand...” |
| 总结当前讨论 | “So what we‘ve agreed on so far is...” |

### 2.3 讨论中的常见话术

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 需要时间思考 | “That’s a great question. Let me think about it for a moment.” |
| 需要更多信息 | “I‘d like to hear more about...” |
| 把问题抛回 | “What does everyone else think?” |
| 结束发言 | “That’s all I wanted to say on this.” / “I think I‘ve covered everything.” |


## 三、英文演讲

### 3.1 演讲的结构

| 部分 | 时间占比 | 核心功能 | 关键动作 |
|:-----|:--------:|:---------|:---------|
| **Opening** | 10% | 抓住注意力 | 数据/故事/问题开场 |
| **Agenda** | 5% | 给出路线图 | “I’ll cover three things...” |
| **Main Body** | 70% | 展开论点 | 每个论点：主题句→数据→分析 |
| **Summary** | 10% | 回顾核心 | 重申2-3个关键点 |
| **Call to Action** | 5% | 推动行动 | 明确下一步 |

### 3.2 开场

| 开场方式 | 句式示例 |
|:---------|:---------|
| 数据开场 | “In the past 12 months, our revenue has grown by 35%.” |
| 问题开场 | “What if we could double our market share in 18 months?” |
| 故事开场 | “Let me start with a story about one of our customers...” |
| 观点开场 | “I believe the next five years will be the most transformative in our industry.” |

### 3.3 路线图

> “Today, I’m going to cover three things. First, I‘ll walk you through our Q3 performance. Second, I’ll highlight the key drivers behind our growth. And finally, I‘ll share our Q4 priorities and what they mean for you.”

### 3.4 主体部分的过渡

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 结束一部分 | “That covers the first part.” |
| 开始新部分 | “Now let’s move on to the second part...” |
| 深入细节 | “Let me drill down into this a bit more.” |
| 回到大图景 | “Stepping back to the bigger picture...” |
| 引入图表 | “Let‘s take a look at this chart, which shows...” |

### 3.5 数据呈现

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 引入数据 | “The data shows that...” / “If you look at this chart...” |
| 强调关键数字 | “What stands out is...” / “The most striking figure is...” |
| 解释趋势 | “What’s interesting about this trend is...” |
| 得出结论 | “What this tells us is...” |

### 3.6 结尾

| 部分 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 总结 | “To sum up, I‘ve covered three key points...” |
| 重申核心 | “The bottom line is...” / “The key takeaway is...” |
| 行动呼吁 | “I encourage you to...” / “I’d like to propose that we...” |
| 开放问答 | “That concludes my presentation. I‘m happy to take any questions.” |


## 四、即兴发言

即兴发言是商务场景中最具挑战性的口语能力——被临时点名发言时，需要在30秒内组织思路并开口。

### 4.1 即兴发言的PREP框架

| 要素 | 内容 | 示例 |
|:----:|:-----|:-----|
| **P**oint | 核心观点 | “I believe we should focus on retention first.” |
| **R**eason | 理由 | “Because improving retention by 5% increases LTV by 25%.” |
| **E**xample | 例证 | “For example, we saw this last quarter when we implemented...” |
| **P**oint | 重申观点 | “So my recommendation is to prioritize retention.” |

### 4.2 即兴发言的话术

被点名时，用这些话术争取思考时间：

| 话术 | 作用 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “Great question. Here‘s my take...” | 肯定问题 + 准备回应 |
| “I’d like to approach this from three angles...” | 明确结构 + 给自己思考空间 |
| “Let me think about it this way...” | 争取思考时间 |
| “If I could just share my perspective...” | 礼貌引入 |


## 二、商业场景还原

### 场景1：会议发言示例

**情境**：项目会上讨论“是否应延期上线来提升质量”。

> “I think we should delay the launch by two weeks. The main reason is that our user testing revealed several critical issues that would impact the user experience.
>
> For example, the onboarding flow is currently taking an average of 4 minutes — nearly double our target. If we launch with this issue, we risk high early churn.
>
> So my view is that a two-week delay is worth it to ensure a high-quality launch and better retention in the long run.”

### 场景2：演讲开场示例

**情境**：向管理团队汇报Q3业绩。

> “Good morning everyone. Thanks for making time today.
>
> In the first half of this year, we were facing flat growth and rising costs. The question was: how do we turn this around?
>
> I’m happy to report that Q3 performance tells a different story. Revenue grew 15% year-over-year, with East China leading the way at 22% growth.
>
> Over the next 15 minutes, I’ll walk you through three key drivers of this turnaround, and then we‘ll discuss the implications for Q4.”

### 场景3：即兴发言示例

**情境**：会议上被临时点名：“John, what do you think about the new marketing campaign?”

> “Great question. I think the campaign is strong, but I’d suggest we adjust the messaging for the younger segment.
>
> The reason is that the current creative resonates well with our existing customers, but it might not connect with Gen Z users who don‘t know our brand yet.
>
> For example, we tested two versions of the ad on social media, and the version with shorter, more direct copy performed 30% better among younger users.
>
> So my suggestion is to run two versions of the campaign — one for our existing customers and one tailored for Gen Z.”


## 三、发音与流畅度提示

### 3.1 常见商务词汇发音

| 词汇 | 正确发音 | 常见错误 |
|:-----|:---------|:---------|
| **analysis** | /əˈnæl.ə.sɪs/（重音在第2音节）| 重音位置错误 |
| **analytics** | /ˌæn.əˈlɪt.ɪks/（重音在第3音节）| — |
| **strategy** | /ˈstræt.ə.dʒi/（重音在第1音节）| — |
| **financial** | /faɪˈnæn.ʃəl/（重音在第2音节）| — |
| **e-commerce** | /ˈiːˌkɑː.mɝːs/ | — |
| **percentage** | /pərˈsen.tɪdʒ/（重音在第2音节）| — |
| **relevant** | /ˈrel.ə.vənt/（重音在第1音节）| — |

### 3.2 语速控制技巧

| 技巧 | 操作 | 效果 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| **关键处放慢** | 在关键数字和结论处放慢语速 | 让听众抓住重点 |
| **善用停顿** | 讲完一个核心观点后停顿2秒 | 给听众消化时间 |
| **变化节奏** | 背景信息快些，重要内容慢些 | 避免单调 |
| **控制填充词** | 减少“um”“like”“you know” | 听起来更专业 |


## 四、常见口语错误与修正

| ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 | 说明 |
|:--------|:--------|:-----|
| “I think we should to focus...” | “I think we should focus...” | 情态动词后不加to |
| “According to me...” | “In my view...” / “From my perspective...” | “according to”不用于第一人称自我引用 |
| “I am agree with you.” | “I agree with you.” | agree是动词，不需要am |
| “The data shows that we have 15% growth.” | “The data shows 15% growth.” | 简洁表达 |
| “We need to discuss about this issue.” | “We need to discuss this issue.” | discuss是及物动词，不加about |
| “I will revert back to you.” | “I will revert to you.” | revert已经包含“back”的意思 |


## 五、实战自测题

- [ ] **会议发言练习**：准备一段1分钟的英文会议发言，主题是“建议公司投入更多资源到用户留存”。用“观点→理由→例证→结论”的结构。

- [ ] **即兴发言练习**：找一个商业话题（如“AI会对我们这个行业产生什么影响”），用PREP框架准备一段1分钟的即兴发言，给自己30秒准备时间，然后录音并回放检查。

- [ ] **演讲开场练习**：准备一个主题为“Q4增长策略”的英文演讲开场，包含：开场吸引 + 路线图。时长30-45秒。

- [ ] **录音自评**：录一段3分钟的英文发言，回放并检查：语速是否合适？关键处有没有放慢？有没有过多的填充词？发音是否清晰？
