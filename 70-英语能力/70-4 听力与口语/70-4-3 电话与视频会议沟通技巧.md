**定位**：电话与视频会议是商科生在国际化工作场景中最具挑战性的沟通形式——缺乏视觉线索、技术问题频发、多人同时在线、时差与背景噪音交织。这个文件帮你建立“怎么说”和“怎么应对”的实操工具箱：会前准备、开场确认、发言技巧、技术问题处理、会议收尾与跟进。不是教你“背话术”，而是让你在不同会议场景中“说得清、听得准、接得住”。

**适用场景**：参加跨国电话会议、视频会议、远程客户沟通、跨团队虚拟协作。


## 一、电话会议的核心挑战

电话会议比面对面会议更难，因为缺乏视觉线索，无法通过口型、手势、表情辅助理解。

### 1.1 五大挑战与应对

| 挑战 | 表现 | 应对策略 |
|:-----|:-----|:---------|
| **无法看到对方** | 难以判断对方是否理解、是否准备发言 | 多用确认句式、主动询问 |
| **声音质量** | 听不清、有回音、延迟 | 使用耳机、靠近麦克风、主动确认 |
| **多人同时说话** | 声音重叠、无法分辨 | 礼貌请求、有序发言 |
| **注意力分散** | 容易走神、遗漏信息 | 做笔记、主动参与 |
| **背景噪音** | 干扰听力和发言 | 主动静音、选择安静环境 |


## 二、会前准备

### 2.1 技术准备

| 检查项 | 操作 |
|:-------|:-----|
| 设备 | 耳机/麦克风是否正常？摄像头是否工作？|
| 网络 | 网络是否稳定？备用连接方案？|
| 平台 | Zoom/Teams/Google Meet/Webex是否已安装并登录？|
| 环境 | 背景是否整洁？光线是否充足？是否安静？|
| 材料 | 会议材料是否已准备好？是否已提前阅读？|

### 2.2 会议前的确认句式

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 确认会议时间 | “Just to confirm, we’re meeting at 3 PM EST tomorrow, correct?” |
| 确认会议链接 | “Could you please share the meeting link again?” |
| 确认议程 | “Is there an agenda for the meeting that I should review beforehand?” |
| 确认参会人 | “Who else will be joining the call?” |

### 2.3 视频会议前的形象检查

| 检查项 | 操作 |
|:-------|:-----|
| 背景 | 虚拟背景或整洁的实体背景 |
| 光线 | 面部光线充足，避免背光 |
| 角度 | 摄像头与眼睛平齐，不要俯拍或仰拍 |
| 着装 | 商务或商务休闲（视公司文化而定）|


## 三、开场与连接确认

### 3.1 会议开场

| 角色 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 主持人 | “Good morning/afternoon everyone. Thanks for joining.” |
| 主持人 | “Let‘s get started. Here’s what we‘ll cover today...” |
| 参与者 | “Good morning, this is [Name] from [Team/Company].” |
| 参与者 | “Hi everyone, thanks for having me.” |

### 3.2 连接确认

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 确认声音 | “Can everyone hear me clearly?” |
| 确认视频 | “Can everyone see my screen?” |
| 确认共享 | “Is everyone able to see the shared screen?” |
| 确认连接 | “I think we might have lost [Name]. Can you hear us?” |

### 3.3 视频会议开场示例

> “Good morning everyone. Thanks for joining today’s call. This is [Name] from the product team, and I‘m joined by [Colleague Name] from marketing.”
> 
> “Before we start, can everyone confirm they can hear me clearly and see the shared screen? Please type ‘yes’ in the chat if everything is working.”


## 四、发言与互动

### 4.1 视频会议中的发言技巧

| 技巧 | 操作 | 效果 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| **看摄像头** | 发言时看摄像头，而非看屏幕上的自己 | 让对方感觉你在“看”他们 |
| **肢体语言** | 适当点头、微笑、手势 | 增强表达的感染力 |
| **不遮挡面部** | 保持面部清晰可见 | 增强信任感 |
| **控制音量** | 保持稳定音量，不要突然大声或小声 | 提高可听性 |
| **使用静音** | 不发言时保持静音 | 减少背景噪音 |

### 4.2 发言句式

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 请求发言 | “I’d like to add something here.” |
| 开始发言 | “From my perspective...” / “I think we should...” |
| 结束发言 | “That’s all I wanted to say on this.” |
| 示意他人发言 | “I think [Name] also has some thoughts on this.” |

### 4.3 回应他人

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 同意 | “I completely agree with [Name].” |
| 部分同意 | “I see [Name]‘s point, but I think...” |
| 需要澄清 | “Could you clarify what you meant by...?” |
| 确认理解 | “So what you’re saying is...” |

### 4.4 处理多人同时发言

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 礼貌打断 | “Sorry, I didn‘t catch that — could you repeat it?” |
| 请求有序 | “Let’s go one at a time. [Name], you go first.” |
| 确认顺序 | “I think [Name] was next. [Name], go ahead.” |


## 五、处理技术问题

### 5.1 音频问题

| 问题 | 句式 | 应对 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 听不清 | “I’m sorry, I didn‘t quite catch that. Could you repeat it?” | 请求重复 |
| 声音断断续续 | “I think we’re having a connection issue. Can you hear me now?” | 检查网络 |
| 背景噪音 | “Could you please mute your microphone?” | 请求静音 |
| 自己听不到 | “I can‘t hear anyone. Let me check my audio settings.” | 检查设备 |
| 自己声音卡顿 | “Let me rejoin the call — I think my connection is unstable.” | 重新加入 |

### 5.2 视频问题

| 问题 | 句式 | 应对 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 看不到画面 | “I can’t see your video. Could you try turning it on again?” | 请求重开 |
| 画面卡顿 | “I‘m going to turn off my video to improve the connection.” | 关视频保音频 |
| 共享屏幕不显示 | “Can everyone see my screen? I’m sharing now.” | 确认可见性 |
| 共享屏幕延迟 | “Let me stop sharing and try again.” | 重新共享 |

### 5.3 应对断线

| 场景 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 重新加入 | “I‘m sorry, I got disconnected. Could you please repeat what I missed?” |
| 确认之前的讨论 | “Just to catch up, what did I miss while I was disconnected?” |
| 确认行动项 | “Before I was disconnected, we were discussing...” |


## 六、会议结束与跟进

### 6.1 确认行动项

| 句式 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|
| “Let me confirm the action items...” | “Let me confirm the action items: John will send the report by Friday, and I’ll prepare the presentation for next week.” |
| “So we‘ve agreed that...” | “So we’ve agreed that we‘ll proceed with Plan A and revisit in Q4.” |
| “Just to summarize today’s decisions...” | “Just to summarize today‘s decisions: we’re launching in October, budget is approved, and we need to finalize the design by next Monday.” |

### 6.2 会议收尾

| 角色 | 句式 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 主持人 | “Thanks everyone for your time and input.” |
| 参与者 | “Thank you for the productive discussion.” |
| 主持人 | “I’ll send out the meeting minutes by the end of today.” |
| 参与者 | “Looking forward to our next meeting.” |

### 6.3 会后跟进邮件

**会议纪要邮件示例**：

> **Subject**: Meeting Minutes – [Project Name] – [Date]
>
> Hi everyone,
>
> Thanks for joining today‘s meeting. Here’s a quick summary of what we covered and the action items:
>
> **Key decisions**:
> - We will launch the new feature on October 15.
> - Budget of $50K approved for the marketing campaign.
>
> **Action items**:
>
> | Action Item | Owner | Deadline |
> |:------------|:-----:|:--------:|
> | Finalize product requirements | John | Aug 15 |
> | Prepare marketing plan | Sarah | Aug 20 |
> | Review budget allocation | Team | Aug 25 |
>
> Please let me know if I missed anything or if you have any questions.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## 二、商业场景还原

### 场景1：电话会议开场

**情境**：你作为新人加入一个跨国项目电话会议。

> **开场话术**：
> “Good morning everyone. This is [Name] from the Shanghai team. I’m the new product analyst on this project. I can hear everyone clearly. Looking forward to working with you all.”

### 场景2：视频会议的技术问题

**情境**：你在视频会议中共享屏幕，但对方看不到。

> **应对话术**：
> “I‘m sharing my screen now. Can everyone see it? — Okay, I see two people said yes and one said no. Let me stop sharing and try again. — How about now? Can everyone see the document?”

### 场景3：电话会议中没听清

**情境**：电话会议中，关键信息被噪音掩盖了。

> **确认话术**：
> “I’m sorry, I think there was some background noise and I missed the last part. Could you please repeat what you said about the timeline?”

### 场景4：处理跨时区会议

**情境**：会议时间对不同时区的人不友好。

> **话术**：
> “I know this meeting time isn‘t ideal for everyone, especially for our colleagues in Europe. I appreciate everyone making time for this. If there are any scheduling issues, please let me know and we can try to rotate meeting times.”


## 三、常见错误与避坑

| 错误 | 正确做法 | 说明 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| 不发言时不静音 | 不发言时保持静音 | 背景噪音会干扰会议 |
| 发言时不看摄像头 | 发言时看摄像头 | 建立眼神连接 |
| 共享屏幕前不确认 | 共享前先问“Can everyone see my screen?” | 避免无效共享 |
| 不记录行动项 | 会议中记录关键决策和行动项 | 会后无法跟进 |
| 断线重连后不确认 | 重连后问“What did I miss?” | 确保信息完整 |


## 四、实战自测题

- [ ] **角色扮演练习**：找一个练习伙伴，模拟一次10分钟的跨国项目电话会议，包含：开场自我介绍、讨论一个议题、处理一次技术问题、确认行动项、会议收尾。

- [ ] **技术问题应对练习**：录制一段视频会议，然后在会议中制造一个“技术问题”（如假装断线、假装听不清），练习用英语应对并恢复会议进程。

- [ ] **断线重连练习**：在模拟会议中途主动“断线”，30秒后“重连”，用英语询问错过了什么内容，并确认当前讨论状态。

- [ ] **会议纪要练习**：参加一次真实的英文会议（或听一段会议录音），用笔记模板记录核心决策和行动项，然后写一封英文会议纪要邮件。
