# C44 · Crunch Product — Syllabus

**Format:** 12 weeks · self-paced · open-source (GPL-3.0). Each week = 3 lectures, 3 exercises, 2 challenges, a mini-project, homework, and a quiz.

**Prerequisites:** You can write plain English clearly and run basic commands in a terminal. No product, design, or analytics background assumed. The analytics weeks use SQL — [C33 Crunch SQL](../C33-CRUNCH-SQL/) helps but is not required; the course teaches the queries you need from scratch.

**Assessment (honor-based):** tick each lesson complete in the reader; finish the weekly mini-project and quiz. A certificate is issued on 100% completion to One-Stop members.

| Week | Theme | Mini-project |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | PM foundations — role, lifecycle, value/viability/feasibility | Teardown of a product you use: role, users, and bets |
| 2 | User research & discovery — interviews, surveys, usability | Run 5 user interviews and synthesize the findings |
| 3 | Problem & opportunity framing — JTBD, sizing, problem statements | Write a validated problem brief with an opportunity size |
| 4 | PRDs & specs — stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases | Write a shippable PRD for one feature |
| 5 | Prioritization & roadmapping — RICE, Kano, cost-of-delay | Prioritize a backlog and build a quarter roadmap |
| 6 | Product analytics in SQL — funnels, cohorts, activation, DAU/MAU | Answer 15 product questions from the event tables |
| 7 | Experimentation — A/B design, sample size, significance | Design and analyze an A/B test from a seed dataset |
| 8 | UX & design collaboration — flows, critique, trade-offs | Critique and redesign a broken flow with a spec |
| 9 | Go-to-market & launch — positioning, launch plan, GA | Write a full launch plan for a v1 feature |
| 10 | Pricing & growth — models, packaging, loops, North Star | Model a pricing change and a growth loop in SQL |
| 11 | AI & LLM product management — scoping, evals, guardrails, cost | Spec + eval an LLM feature with a failure plan |
| 12 | Capstone — zero to one: discover, spec, measure, launch | Take a product from blank page to a launched v1 |

**Outcome:** run discovery, write the spec, prove it with SQL analytics and a clean experiment, ship it — and lead a product from zero to one, including AI features.
