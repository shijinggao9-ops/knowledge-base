# Week 11 Challenges

Two open-ended problems. Unlike the exercises, there is **no single right answer** here — the point is to make a defensible judgment call under real constraints, state your reasoning, and be honest about the tradeoffs you're accepting. Budget ~90 minutes each.

| 2 | [Cost-Cap an AI Feature Without Breaking It](challenge-02-cost-cap-an-ai-feature.md) | Medium–Hard | Cost controls under a real traffic spike, without wrecking the UX (Lecture 3) |

## How these are graded

Neither challenge has an answer key. Each ships with a rubric table describing what separates a weak submission from a strong one — read it *before* you start, not after. The strongest submissions in this course consistently share three traits: they **state their interpretation explicitly**, they **cite specific evidence** rather than asserting a conclusion, and they **name at least one alternative view** they considered and rejected. A confident answer with no stated reasoning is a weaker submission than a hedged answer that shows its work — that's doubly true this week, where "the model is smart" is never an acceptable substitute for a stated guardrail or a cited number.
