# Week 1 Challenges

Two open-ended problems. Unlike the exercises, there is **no single right answer** here — the point is to make a defensible judgment call, state your reasoning, and be honest about what you don't know. Budget ~60–90 minutes each.

| # | Challenge | Difficulty | Practices |
|--:|-----------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | [Draw the PM/PO/Eng-Lead Responsibility Line](challenge-01-pm-vs-po-role-debate.md) | Medium | Role boundaries under ambiguity (Lecture 1) |
| 2 | [Diagnose a Real Product's Lifecycle Stage](challenge-02-lifecycle-stage-diagnosis.md) | Medium–Hard | Lifecycle diagnosis from public, imperfect evidence (Lecture 3) |

## How these are graded

Neither challenge has an answer key. Each ships with a rubric table describing what separates a weak submission from a strong one — read it *before* you start, not after. The strongest submissions in this course consistently share three traits: they **state their interpretation explicitly**, they **cite specific evidence** rather than asserting a conclusion, and they **name at least one alternative view** they considered and rejected. A confident answer with no stated reasoning is a weaker submission than a hedged answer that shows its work.
