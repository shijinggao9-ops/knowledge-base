# C44 · Crunch Product — Brand Guide

- **Accent:** Code Crunch blue `#2463EB` (with deep `#1d4ed8`).
- **Voice:** decisive and evidence-first. Always start from the user's problem, then the data, then the bet. No feature ships without a "why now" and a metric that will tell you if it worked. Opinions are cheap; validated problems are the product.
- **Icon motif:** a discovery magnifier over a funnel chart.
- **One-liner:** "Build products people want — discovery, metrics, and AI, from idea to launch."
