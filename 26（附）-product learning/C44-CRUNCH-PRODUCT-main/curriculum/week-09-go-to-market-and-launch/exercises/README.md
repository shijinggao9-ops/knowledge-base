# Week 9 Exercises — Overview

Three exercises, done in order, each building on the last. All three work against the **Guest & External Collaborator Access** launch — the positioning you write in Exercise 1 feeds the checklist you build in Exercise 2, and the checklist's success-metrics section is exactly what Exercise 3's SQL answers.

| # | Exercise | Skill | Time |
|--:|----------|-------|-----:|
| 1 | [exercise-01-write-a-positioning-statement.md](./exercise-01-write-a-positioning-statement.md) | Positioning statements, feature-benefit-proof mapping | 1.5h |
| 2 | [exercise-02-build-a-launch-checklist.md](./exercise-02-build-a-launch-checklist.md) | Cross-functional checklist, launch tier selection, RACI | 1.5h |
| 3 | [exercise-03-define-launch-success-in-sql.md](./exercise-03-define-launch-success-in-sql.md) | SQL launch dashboard, rollback-trigger query | 1.5h |

## How to work these

- **Set up the seed data first.** All three exercises query the tables from the [week README](../README.md) — `orgs`, `rollout_exposures`, `guest_invitations`, `support_tickets`. Confirm the four sanity-check counts (24, 24, 32, 16) before starting.
- **Write real prose, not fragments.** Exercise 1 and 2 are judged on whether a real stakeholder (a Sales VP, a Support lead) could act on what you wrote — not on hitting a word count.
- **Run every query in Exercise 3.** SQL that "looks right" but returns the wrong row count is wrong. Check your results against the "Expected" notes.
- **Keep your work.** Exercise 1's positioning statements and Exercise 2's checklist are direct inputs to the mini-project — you are not starting from scratch on Saturday.

## Submission

Commit all three exercise deliverables to your portfolio under `c44-week-09/exercise-01/`, `exercise-02/`, and `exercise-03/` respectively.
