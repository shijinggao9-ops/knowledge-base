# Week 10 Challenges

Two open-ended problems. Unlike the exercises, there is **no single right answer** here — the point is to make a defensible judgment call, back it with this week's numbers, and be honest about what you don't know. Budget ~90 minutes each.

| # | Challenge | Difficulty | Practices |
|--:|-----------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | [Reprice a Product Without Mass Churn](./challenge-01-reprice-without-churning.md) | Medium–Hard | Rollout design, risk mitigation, grandfathering (Lecture 1 + Lecture 3) |
| 2 | [Find the Highest-Leverage Growth Loop](./challenge-02-find-the-highest-leverage-loop.md) | Medium | Loop vs. funnel leverage comparison, CAC-adjusted reasoning (Lecture 2 + Lecture 3) |

## How these are graded

Neither challenge has an answer key. Each ships with a rubric table describing what separates a weak submission from a strong one — read it *before* you start, not after. The strongest submissions in this course consistently share three traits: they **state their interpretation explicitly**, they **cite specific numbers from this week's seed data** rather than asserting a conclusion, and they **name at least one alternative approach** they considered and rejected. A confident recommendation with no numbers behind it is a weaker submission than a hedged one that shows its work.
