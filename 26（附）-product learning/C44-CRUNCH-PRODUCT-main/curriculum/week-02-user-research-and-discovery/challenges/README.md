# Week 2 — Challenges

Two open-ended problems. Unlike the exercises, these have **no single right answer** — the skill is judgment, not recall. Do them after the three exercises.

1. **[Challenge 1 — Recruit a Hard-to-Reach Segment](challenge-01-recruit-a-hard-to-reach-segment.md)** — plan recruiting for Shiftly's night-shift warehouse workers, a segment that's genuinely difficult to reach and easy to recruit badly. *(~60 min.)*
2. **[Challenge 2 — Separate Signal from Noise](challenge-02-separate-signal-from-noise.md)** — sort 20 raw pieces of user feedback into real validated needs versus surface-level feature requests, and defend each call. *(~60 min.)*

## How these are judged

There's no answer key with a single correct sort or a single correct recruiting plan. Instead, each challenge tells you what a *strong* submission looks like. You're being graded on:

- **Explicit reasoning** — can you justify why you made the recruiting or categorization call you made?
- **Awareness of bias** — did you notice and address a way your plan or your sort could be skewed, before someone else pointed it out?
- **Honesty about limits** — do you flag what you genuinely can't determine from the information given, rather than papering over it with confidence?

Keep your work in `challenge-01.md` / `challenge-02.md` with your plan or sort **and** your written reasoning. The reasoning is the point.
